# Ecosystem

English · [Русский](ECOSYSTEM.ru.md)

> JealousVault is an open project, not a gated product. Its ecosystem is open to independent developers, third-party extensions and alternative implementations. Commercial extensions are welcome, and their authors retain ownership of their respective work.

## The public product

The public product is the complete JealousVault backup tool, licensed under Apache-2.0.

- It is not a crippled freemium edition and has no feature gates.
- Restore, integrity checks, security fixes, basic alerts and documentation are part of it and stay available to everyone.
- Anyone may use it, fork it, modify it and run it on any number of hosts, including commercially.
- Alternative implementations and compatible products are allowed by the licence. The project does not endorse them and is not responsible for them.

## Extensions and plugins

- A plugin or extension is a separate work. Its author chooses the licence, including GPL-3.0.
- Commercial extensions are welcome, and their authors retain ownership of their work.
- A third-party extension must not claim to be official or endorsed by JealousVault without permission. It may say that it is compatible with JealousVault.
- The documented CLI, config and JSON output are the stable integration surface. Internal packages and undocumented behaviour may change; see [STABILITY.md](STABILITY.md).
- The project may publish its own commercial extensions, plugins and hosted services. They are additive: they do not remove capabilities from the public product.

## Commercial support and custom work

Paid consulting, priority support, custom development, hosted services and commercial licences for project-owned extensions are separate offerings. They fund the work on the public product. Buying them is optional; the public product remains usable without them. Commercial licences for project-owned extensions are granted by mnnoee. Individual commercial terms are negotiated privately.

## Contributions

- Contributions to the public repository need a DCO sign-off and are accepted under Apache-2.0. They do not grant additional proprietary rights. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Code that mnnoee accepts into a commercial extension is covered by a separate written agreement, [CLA.md](CLA.md), only when both parties explicitly agree.
- A later agreement does not revoke the licence already granted for earlier contributions.

## Name and logo

The JealousVault name and logo identify the project. A fork, plugin or compatible product may describe itself as compatible with JealousVault, but must not imply that it is the official project or that it is endorsed by it without written permission.
