# Code of conduct

English · [Русский](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ru.md)

## Commitment

Participation in the project should be free from harassment for everyone, regardless of experience, age, appearance, nationality, language, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity, health or skill level.

## Expected behaviour

- discuss code and decisions, not people;
- give specific feedback and explain why;
- admit mistakes and fix them;
- respect boundaries and other people's time;
- help newcomers understand restic, systemd and restore workflows.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- insults, threats, bullying or stalking;
- sexist, racist or other discriminatory statements;
- publishing other people's personal data without permission;
- trolling, spam or deliberately derailing discussions;
- publishing secrets, passwords, keys or other people's backup contents.

## Scope

This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, chats and project-related events.

## Reporting

Email **vault@jealousmoon.com** with the subject `[CONDUCT] JealousVault` (personal fallback: **me@mnnoee.ru**). Reports are handled confidentially. Describe what happened, where, and who was affected; include links or screenshots if they do not expose other people's secrets.

## Consequences

The maintainer may:

1. ask the person to stop the unacceptable behaviour;
2. remove a comment or PR and issue a warning;
3. temporarily or permanently restrict participation;
4. report serious cases to the platform.

The maintainer reviews each report on the facts and does not disclose the reporter's identity unless necessary.
