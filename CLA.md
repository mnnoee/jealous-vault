# Commercial extension contributor licence agreement

English · [Русский](CLA.ru.md)

This is a template for a **separate written agreement**. It applies only when mnnoee explicitly
invites a contributor to submit code for a commercial extension and both parties accept it in
writing.

It does **not** apply to ordinary pull requests to the public Apache-2.0 repository. Those are
governed by [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the Developer Certificate of Origin, and they do
not grant any additional proprietary rights.

This document is a template, not legal advice. The signed agreement may add governing law, venue,
payment, support and other terms.

## Parties

- **Licensor**: the individual or legal entity named in the signature block, who owns or controls
  the rights in the Contribution.
- **Licensee**: **mnnoee**, acting under the project name JealousVault, and any entity to which the
  Licensee assigns this agreement under section 9.

## 1. Definitions

- **Contribution**: code, documentation, tests, designs or other material that the Licensor
  intentionally submits for inclusion in a Commercial Extension and that the Licensee accepts in
  writing.
- **Commercial Extension**: a paid or otherwise commercially distributed plugin, extension,
  integration or edition that is separate from the public Apache-2.0 product.

## 2. Scope and exclusions

This agreement covers only the Contribution accepted in writing by the Licensee.

It does **not** cover:

- material created by the Licensor before contributing to the project;
- third-party code, libraries, assets, data or documentation;
- material the Licensor has no legal right to submit;
- files or parts explicitly marked `Not a Contribution`;
- anything the Licensee has not accepted in writing.

The Licensor must identify any third-party material included in the Contribution and its licence
before submission.
